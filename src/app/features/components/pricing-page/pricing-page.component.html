FAQ

Questions about pricing

Can I start with the free plan?

Yes. The free plan is designed to help you test SignFlow and send your first client flow before upgrading.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel whenever you want, and your paid plan will stay active until the end of your billing period.

What counts as a completed submission?

A completed submission is a client flow that has been filled, accepted, and signed successfully.

Will more business features be added later?

Yes. Team features, branding controls, and more advanced workflow tools can be added as SignFlow grows.
