Cookie Policy

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us improve your experience and understand how our website is used.

Types of cookies we use

Essential cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly and cannot be disabled.

Analytics cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website, such as which pages are visited and how long users stay.

We use Google Analytics for this purpose. These cookies are only activated with your consent.

Marketing cookies

We may use marketing cookies to provide more relevant content and advertising. These are only enabled with your consent.

Third-party services

These services may set their own cookies.

Consent

When you first visit our website, you will be asked to accept or reject non-essential cookies.
